# Brand spec: The Say and Play Space

## Source material
- The Facebook page cover image, profile picture and About details (phone and WhatsApp), and the Instagram bio, supplied by the client as screenshots.
- Instagram (@thesayandplayspace) and Facebook could not be reached from the build environment, so no post images or videos have been pulled yet.

## Positioning (from their bio and cover)
- "Sensory Boxes & Toys. Inspired by paediatric therapists. Stimulating growth, learning and fun one toy at a time."
- "Hands-on, open-ended sensory experiences designed to inspire imagination and support your child's development."
- Pillars: Fine motor skills · Language development · Creativity & imagination · Social skills & turn-taking · Sensory exploration
- Taglines: "Explore · Play · Learn · Discover" and "Play today, grow tomorrow!"
- Voice: warm, parent-friendly, encouraging and simple. The client's Instagram bio uses a few emoji; the website uses SVG icons instead, as StratStudios rules require.

## Type
- Display: **Fredoka** 500/600, a rounded geometric typeface close to the logo wordmark and the cover's pill labels
- Body: **Nunito** 400–800

## Shape and motion
- Organic, morphing blob photo frames to echo the cover's cut-out photo shapes
- Pill buttons, 24–36px card radii and soft teal-tinted shadows
- Twinkling four-point sparkles and a dashed "bee trail" taken from the cover illustrations
- An "Explore · Play · Learn · Discover" marquee ribbon
- Gentle scroll reveals that respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
