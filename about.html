Our story

It starts with noticing.

Hasies en Gansies was created around a simple idea: the things around children should feel as considered as the moments they become part of.

We believe in pieces that are useful without losing their charm, gifts that feel personal, and a shopping experience that feels more like a thoughtful recommendation than a rushed transaction.

Our collection is growing with care. The details may change, but the feeling stays the same: warm, gentle and made for real life.
